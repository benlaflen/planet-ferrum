Version: 0.1.3
  Features:
    - Enabled manually inserting robots into fabricator.
    - Added catalyzed iron plates as an intermediary between iron plates and molten iron.
  Balancing:
    - Rebalanced iron melting to make a loop far less desirable (and require obscene amounts of productivity).

  Bugfixes:
    - Fixed localization issues with habitability and frontline robotics.
    - Fixed issues with recycling overriding vanilla recycling variants.
  Locale:
    - Put all names in proper sentence case.
